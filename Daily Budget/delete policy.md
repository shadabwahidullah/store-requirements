**Data Deletion Policy**
DailyBudget stores all your financial data locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers or cloud services. Your information remains completely private and under your control at all times.

How to Delete Your Data
To permanently delete all your data from DailyBudget:

- Open your device's Settings app
- Navigate to Apps (or Application Manager)
- Find and tap on DailyBudget
- Tap on Storage or Storage & Cache
- Tap on Clear Data or Clear Storage
- This will immediately remove all your transactions, budgets, categories, and settings from the app.

**Alternative: Uninstall the App**

Uninstalling DailyBudget from your device will also permanently delete all your data, as it is stored locally:

Android: Long-press the DailyBudget icon → Uninstall

iOS: Long-press the DailyBudget icon → Remove App → Delete App

**Data Recovery**
Once your data is deleted (either by clearing storage or uninstalling), it cannot be recovered. If you want to keep a backup before deletion, we recommend exporting your data through the app's export feature (if available) or taking screenshots of important information.
